FAQs
What is a Vellum assistant?
Vellum is a personal, secure AI assistant that you fully own and customize to your needs. It runs locally on your machine or in the cloud, is available around the clock from any device, remembers you over time, manages tasks across your tools, and handles work for you automatically.How is my Vellum assistant different from ChatGPT or Claude?
ChatGPT and Claude are chat windows that only act on command, train off your data, and have limited memory. Vellum is an autonomous assistant with long-term memory that takes real actions across your files and tools. Your personal data stays private, none of your data accessible by Vellum unless you opt in, and you can switch between models including OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or open weights via Ollama at any time.What can my Vellum assistant do?
It can send emails, manage your calendar, browse the web, create slides, write code, run deep research, monitor websites, and execute recurring tasks in the background. It comes with built-in skills for Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, and your filesystem, and can be extended with community plugins or your own custom skills.Can my Vellum assistant do things without asking me?
Only what you allow it to. Vellum has a fine-grained permissions system built to protect your data. High-stakes actions like sending emails to new contacts, modifying important files, or spending credits require explicit approval until you mark them as trusted. You are always in control.What can I use my Vellum assistant on?
Vellum is native to Mac and accessible everywhere: iOS, Android, web app, voice, email, Telegram, Slack, and terminal. Your assistant shares the same memory, active tasks, and schedules across every surface.Is the data from my Vellum assistant private?
Yes. By default, your assistant runs locally. Your conversation history, memory, and credentials stay on your machine and are never used to train models. Vellum is open-source, so you can inspect the code yourself. If you use Vellum Cloud, your assistant runs in an isolated container provisioned exclusively for you.How much does my Vellum assistant cost?
Vellum is free to download and start. If you choose Vellum Cloud, subscription plans start at $30/month (Mighty), $100/month (Super), or $200/month (Ultra) with dedicated compute, storage, and bundled credits. Compare every plan on the pricing plans page.
