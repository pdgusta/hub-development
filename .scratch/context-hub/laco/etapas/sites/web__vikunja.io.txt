Frequently Asked Questions What is Vikunja? 

Vikunja is an open-source, self-hostable to-do app. It helps you organize your tasks and projects with features like list, kanban, gantt, and table views — all while keeping your data under your control. Is Vikunja free and open source? 

Yes. Vikunja is licensed under the AGPLv3 and completely free to self-host. If you prefer not to manage your own server, Vikunja Cloud offers a paid hosted option. How do I install Vikunja? 

You can install Vikunja using Docker, native binaries for your platform, or sign up for Vikunja Cloud. Use the install wizard for a guided setup. Do I have to self-host Vikunja? 

No. While self-hosting is a great option, Vikunja Cloud gives you a fully managed instance with automatic updates, backups, and zero maintenance. It's the easiest way to get started and directly supports the continued development of Vikunja. Can I import tasks from Todoist, Trello, or Microsoft To-Do? 

Yes. Vikunja has built-in migration support for Todoist, Trello, Microsoft To-Do, and other platforms. You can import your existing tasks and projects directly from the Vikunja interface.
