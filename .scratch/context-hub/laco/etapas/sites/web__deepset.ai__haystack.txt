Frequently asked questions

What is agentic orchestration?

Agentic orchestration is the way teams design how AI agents reason, use context, call tools, and complete multi-step tasks.
‍
Instead of letting an agent operate as a black box, orchestration defines how it should move through a workflow: what information it can use, which tools it can call, when it should route a request, and how it should hand off or escalate. With Haystack Enterprise Platform, these agent workflows are built as explicit, inspectable pipelines, so teams can test, trace, debug, and improve agent behavior before and after deployment.
‍
How do organizations handle governance, access control, and compliance?

The Haystack Enterprise Platform provides role-based access control at two levels, organization and workspace, with preset roles and custom workspace roles for precise control over who can build and access what. Logs are enabled for every pipeline and index by default, supporting audit and troubleshooting. Combined with the open-source foundation, regulated buyers can audit the code itself and demonstrate to a reviewer that the system is under institutional control. The platform is GDPR and ISO 27001 compliant.
What is Haystack?

Haystack is deepset’s open-source AI orchestration framework for building production-ready AI agents, advanced RAG systems, multimodal applications, search, question answering, and other LLM-powered systems. It lets developers structure AI applications as explicit, modular pipelines made of retrievers, routers, memory layers, tools, evaluators, and generators, so each component can be tested, replaced, and improved independently, with full model and data flexibility.
Haystack is designed for a wide range of users, including AI engineers, developers, data scientists, and software developers seeking to build scalable, customizable AI solutions. It caters to technical experts who want fine-grained control over AI workflows and newcomers looking for an intuitive framework to get started with LLM-powered agents and applications.
What is context engineering?

Context engineering is the process of controlling what information, tools, memory, and instructions an AI system can use before it responds or acts.
In RAG and agentic AI, context quality often determines output quality. Haystack Enterprise Platform helps teams design and test how context is retrieved, filtered, ranked, routed, and passed into an AI workflow, so agents can make decisions based on the right information and reduce hallucination.
What is Sovereign AI?

Sovereign AI is AI your organization can build, run, and evolve on your own terms, without being locked into a single vendor, model, or stack.
With deepset, sovereignty means control over what your AI knows, how it acts, where it runs, and how it performs. Use the Haystack Enterprise Platform to build production AI agents on an open, inspectable foundation, deploy them in the environment your organization requires, and keep your architecture portable as models, regulations, and business needs change.
