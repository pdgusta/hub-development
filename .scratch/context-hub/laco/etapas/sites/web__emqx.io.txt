FAQs
Quick answers to the most common questions about EMQX and AI-driven MQTT workflows.

What is EMQX?

EMQX is a unified MQTT platform for real-time AI and IoT data streaming. It connects, processes, and streams data from millions of devices to your cloud, AI, and analytics.

How do I deploy EMQX (Cloud, BYOC, or self-managed)?

EMQX offers three deployment options: Fully managed Cloud (Serverless or Dedicated) for fastest time-to-value; BYOC (Bring Your Own Cloud) for better isolation and data residency; Self-managed (Enterprise) for full control on your own infrastructure. The core MQTT architecture stays consistent across all options.

How does EMQX integrate with AI?

EMQX streams MQTT data to AI agents or ML pipelines for inference and closed-loop control.

What protocols does EMQX support?

EMQX supports MQTT versions 3.1.1 and 5.0, including MQTT over WebSocket and QUIC; as well as NATS, CoAP, LwM2M, and over 100 industrial protocols.

How does EMQX ensure high availability and disaster recovery?

EMQX is designed for mission-critical IoT and AI scenarios with high availability through clustering and fault-tolerant architecture, rolling upgrades to minimize downtime, and disaster recovery options to meet your RTO/RPO targets.

How does EMQX secure device connections and data access?

Security is integrated from the device to the broker: TLS/mTLS encryption in transit, flexible authentication (certs, JWT/OAuth, etc.), fine-grained authorization (ACL/RBAC) controls topic access, and audit-friendly controls for security reviews and compliance.

How does EMQX pricing work?

We offer transparent plans tailored to your production workloads, including deployment model, capacity, and support level. For enterprise procurement, we support security reviews and standard commercial terms.

Is there a free trial for EMQX?

Yes – Download EMQX Enterprise for free or start on EMQX Cloud and upgrade as you grow.
